## Local use, preservation and untested claims
Before this change the seven [aspect questions](ALGORITHM.html#aspects) had no subject profiles or named audience/security/maintenance technique families. They could carry such criteria when declared; absence of names was not proof of impossible coverage. This catalogue makes questions operational with overlapping concerns rather than claiming mathematical independence or empirical superiority.

Protocol loading is an explicit read-and-record obligation within existing framing/assessment, not an implemented skill tool. [Template](REVIEW-TEMPLATE.md) records exact loaded guidance, precedence, applicability and conflicts. No local packaging checker is supplied; documentation inspections must state their coverage and gaps. Actual consumer loading must be witnessed per task.

The existing [supplementary SVG](ALGORITHM.html#graph) restoration was a pre-existing edit: static title/description, labels, six nodes/nine edges and scoped overflow/focus styling had historical static review. Browser/keyboard/AT/zoom/print behavior remains unobserved, not certified. Diagram, style and navigation are preserved independently of new protocol text. Original Musk recording, later Meadows wording, external runtime behavior, comprehensive safety and substantive subject tests remain gaps; no repeated citation turns them into facts.
