SCAR protocol

Strategic · Correctness · Accuracy · Ratchet. Understand what matters, build a coherent increment, verify the evidence and advance only an exact authorized candidate.

Foundational structured environment

Scratchpad

Typically Beads: memory and communication containing intent, status, findings, decisions, uncertainties and evidence references. Not an alternate codebase.

Current state

The exact HEAD of main in the code repository. Authoritative current state, not automatic proof of quality or past acceptance evidence.

History

Git main, trunk-based. Verified authorized commits advance main; corrections append rather than erase earlier decisions.

Candidate

Uncommitted repository changes based on an exact main commit, with exact base and content identity. Transitional, not another durable store.

The repository is the only codebase. Record task scope and preserve unrelated edits. A filename alone does not identify exact content: use a reproducible content digest or exact snapshot of scoped paths/changes, including newly created files and deletions. Evidence names that identity, base, criteria and context. Reconcile main, candidate and scratchpad on resume; justify evidence reuse or refresh. An entry claiming acceptance cannot replace an actual main commit.

Seven review aspects

Define concrete criteria proportionally to the task. Assess all seven aspects for applicability; justify omissions, scope and evidence reuse. Do not conceal a failed required aspect inside an average. Review strength follows task risk, not compulsory recursion. A review target is assessed for the evidence and coverage of its claims, not as though it were the candidate.

Investigate unknowns when useful; a required Unknown blocks acceptance. A declared excluded claim remains a gap, never an established fact.

Adaptive lifecycle

Adaptive understand/change/verify progression is the default where designs collide, especially during high churn. These positions organize work, not rigid runtime stages. Frame proportionally; interleave checks while building; substantively review a coherent candidate, not each edit.

  1. Frame and understand: name intent, scope, required criteria, protected obligations, roles and evidence context. Reconcile exact main baseline, candidate and scratchpad. Rank consequential structures, symmetries and invariants with witnesses and counterexamples before polishing. Local evidence can overturn that priority.
  2. Coherent increment: build a significant, reviewable candidate within intent. Retain exact base/content identity and unrelated edits. Interleave useful checks without treating every edit as a separate substantive review.
  3. Check and assess: run declared checks and evidence-based assessment against applicable aspect criteria. Record scope, results, uncertainties and allegations. Clean means no supported findings within the examined content/context, not universal truth.
  4. Resolve findings and refine: adjudicate allegations as Supported, Unsupported or Unresolved using named decision-maker, criteria and evidence. Repair only supported changes. Continue building at 2 or revisit understanding at 1 if assumptions break; refresh affected evidence/conclusions and justify reuse. Finish unresolved/rejected at 6 when appropriate, or proceed to 5 with current required evidence and resolved required findings.
  5. Accept and advance main: confirm eligibility and protected baseline obligations; designated authority authorizes exact candidate, expected main baseline and evidence/criteria context. Recheck these before the verified authorized commit on main; confirm the main commit matches the candidate before claiming Accepted. If denied, stale or interrupted, record Unresolved at 6 rather than claim acceptance.
  6. Record outcome and select next task: record Accepted, Rejected or Unresolved, exact resulting main HEAD, candidate disposition, findings, uncertainties and evidence references in scratchpad. Edits-only authority ends as verified uncommitted work awaiting authority. Reconcile any interruption before selecting the next task at 1.

Criticism is an allegation, not an automatic edit. Targeted review-of-review is warranted only by concrete doubt: unsupported evidence, contradictions, threatened obligations, disputed interpretation or consequential gaps. Inspect the disputed claim and its consequences; no mandatory review depth or repeated clean passes. Correcting a review may leave candidate content unchanged but invalidate affected conclusions. Straightforward failures need compact disposition, not compulsory separate documents.

Bounded shared protocol credits

Before substantive work, declare a finite positive initial work allocation and a separate finite positive protected return reserve in the task scratchpad, with bounded operation scopes and fixed positive, heterogeneous prices. These are task-specific protocol conventions, not time, energy or production cost defaults. Framing, evidence gathering, orientation, edits, checks, substantive review, justified review descendants, adjudication, revalidation and acceptance attempts all consume work credits. All participants and attempts share the same task account: debit before each operation; failure and interruption retain the charge, with no refunds or silent reset on retry, review, role change or resume. An operation reaching its declared scope limit stops; further work needs another affordable precharged operation.

At 80% of initial work consumed, or before a proposed affordable charge reaches or crosses that threshold, strongly signal prepare-to-return. Record the reason and bounded closure plan; prefer justified closure over expansion. This never relaxes acceptance criteria or authority. Admit only affordable operations within intent. When remaining work is zero or insufficient for the next operation, halt: no new substantive work, including checks, repairs, review, adjudication or acceptance. A lawfully precharged bounded operation may finish; an interrupted acceptance attempt requires honest reconciliation of actual main, not a fictional unchanged baseline.

The return reserve cannot fund substantive work. Predeclare its bounded minimal outcome report, reconciliation of main/candidate identities from existing records, remaining findings/evidence pointers, accounting and upward return. It cannot acquire new evidence, repair content, decide findings or advance main. Preserve main when acceptance has not occurred and keep the exact uncommitted candidate separate. If accounting or identity cannot be reconciled from existing records, report Unknown and stop; do not invent permission to spend. Resume retains initial allocations and every charge, alongside actual main/candidate/evidence reconciliation; any later allowance requires explicit commander authorization and a recorded extension, never erased consumption. The reserve makes bounded reporting affordable, not a guarantee of host availability.

Mission command within the same positions

The commander sets intent, constraints, success criteria and allowance; the executor adapts locally within them, reporting verified effects. Command authority is distinct from designated acceptance authority. In the fleet mapping, Moltke owns tasking and re-decomposition; Hopper executes, evidence/orientation roles inform decisions, and Moltke owns closeout. This is a role mapping, not a compulsory roster or extra stage. Routine adaptation stays local; material strategic Surprise or Opportunity goes upward rather than silently expanding scope or credit.

A material BackBrief records trigger (Surprise | Opportunity), scope (OutsideMission | PackageLevel | SystemLevel), cited observation, intent_relevance, local_action, requested_response (Acknowledge | AdjustIntent | ReDecompose | EscalateToUser) and confidence (high | medium | low). Requests recommend, not authorize. An ordinary planned credit return is an outcome/continuation report, not a fabricated Surprise or Opportunity.

Higher misalignment pauses work for orientation before operator choices. Fleet judgement cues distinguish two repeat rejections on the same defect class from new classes across more than three review rounds or defensive scaffolding exceeding twice core domain logic. Already at round three, halt if layers accumulate to satisfy edge cases. The commander obtains orientation with at least two ranked clean seams and tradeoffs, then presents two to four structured operator choices with a recommendation and re-decomposes after selection. These are doctrine cues, not calibrated SCAR thresholds or runtime proof. Investigation still costs work credits; without them, return the friction record and request a decision, not free analysis.

Evidence authority and ratchet

Name drafter, reviewer, finding decision-maker and designated acceptance authority, recording overlaps and limitations. The drafter cannot self-authorize. Explicit standing authorization may avoid a human decision at each step without weakening exact-content, baseline or evidence requirements. The scratchpad corroborates; it does not confer acceptance.

Changed candidate, baseline, criteria or evidence invalidates affected authorization and conclusions. Confirm protected per-aspect obligations under a compatible comparison context; an increasing average is insufficient. If comparability is unknown, resolve it or separately authorize a changed contract without claiming the old obligations still hold.

Before advancing main, verify exact candidate/base/context and authorized scope, excluding unrelated edits. After committing, confirm exact content and resulting main HEAD. If main moved, do not overwrite it: reconcile the candidate, refresh affected evidence and obtain applicable authorization. If commit confirmation is interrupted or partial, reconcile actual main and scratchpad; do not claim Accepted merely from intent or a record. Rejected and unresolved candidates do not advance main through this protocol.

Pruning removes redundancy, not protected obligations, mandated evidence or history. Mistaken acceptance calls for a new corrective task and an appended authorized commit, not erasure. These are conditional procedural requirements, not implemented security. Under edits-only authority, verified uncommitted work remains pending review and designated commit authority. First acceptance requires every declared required criterion and a compatible protected-obligation comparison, even when main has no past quality evidence; syntax alone is insufficient. Unknown comparability blocks acceptance under the existing contract.
