# 12 — Requirement questioning and protected economy

Status: bounded source inspiration plus existing SCAR economy/adjudication safeguards.

## Mechanism

Question whether a requirement serves consequential intent before optimizing its
implementation. Seek redundant text, duplicated criticism and speculative work.
Simulate deletion against required obligations and evidence before simplifying.

SCAR criticism is adjudicated, not automatically obeyed. Protected pruning removes
redundancy, never mandated evidence, history or per-aspect obligations.
An unsupported requirement can be rejected with rationale; a binding requirement
cannot be silently reclassified as unnecessary.

## Example

Two paragraphs repeat the same witness and qualification. Keep one canonical account
and refer to it. If removing the second paragraph also removes an uncertainty caveat,
the deletion is not yet a valid economy improvement.

## Limits

The historical publisher account of Musk's engineering philosophy orders questioning,
deleting, simplifying/optimizing, accelerating and automating. SCAR imports neither
a deletion quota nor a mandatory five-step sequence. No test-removal mandate follows.
The original recording was not freshly verified in this mission.
