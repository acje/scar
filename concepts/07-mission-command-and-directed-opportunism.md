# 07 — Mission command and directed opportunism

Status: existing SCAR mission-command mechanism plus user-supplied Bungay framing.

## Mechanism

The commander sets intent, constraints, success criteria and allowance.
The executor adapts within them and reports verified effects. Routine adaptation
stays local; material Surprise or Opportunity goes upward, not into silent scope growth.
Command authority is distinct from designated acceptance authority.

A material BackBrief carries trigger, scope, cited observation, intent relevance,
local action, requested response and confidence. A request recommends, not authorizes.
Architectural friction can pause execution for fresh orientation and operator choice;
investigation still consumes bounded shared work.

## Example

Shell quoting breaks a declared check. Correct equivalent quoting locally without
narrowing coverage. If preserving coverage instead requires a new dependency or
changed requirement, report the material constraint to the commander.

## Limits and attribution

Bungay's *The Art of Action* publisher description connects mission command,
empowered initiative and execution/results. “Directed opportunism” appears in a
quoted review there. This supports broad attribution, not a freshly verified full
book model or precise three-gap derivation. SCAR's BackBrief and fleet escalation
thresholds are local adaptations, not universally calibrated Bungay rules.
