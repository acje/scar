# 16 — Quality-diversity / MAP-Elites

Additional idea 2 of 5. Status: external candidate mechanism, not adopted SCAR policy.

## Mechanism

Choose variation dimensions describing qualitatively different solutions.
Maintain a map/archive of high-performing solutions across those descriptors,
rather than retaining only one global optimum. MAP-Elites is the cited exemplar.
The abstract supports this high-level illumination mechanism, not full update rules.

## SCAR relationship

No named behavioral-descriptor elite archive was observed in the surveyed protocol,
template, catalogue, proposals or eight skill bodies. Seven review questions are
obligations to assess, not archive coordinates or a quality-diversity optimizer.
Scoped absence does not rule out every historical or implicit analogue.

## Example

An experiment might retain equally compliant explanation styles indexed by audience
and presentation structure, rather than losing every alternative to one aggregate
score. Descriptors and quality would need explicit task-specific definitions.

## Tradeoffs and limits

An archive can expose diverse useful alternatives but increases storage and
evaluation work; descriptor choice can conceal meaningful differences.
All retained candidates still need protected obligations and applicable authority.
No archive size, search effectiveness or SCAR improvement is established here.
Quality-diversity and GEPA's task-subset Pareto frontier are related but not identical.
