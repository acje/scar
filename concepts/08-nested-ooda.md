# 08 — Nested OODA

Status: user framing and existing discussion proposal, not an adopted protocol stage.

## Mechanism

Observe, Orient, Decide and Act can explain recurring feedback at two scales.
Strategic work concerns consequential intent, assumptions, framing and continuation.
Tactical work concerns coherent increments, check/review observations, interpretation,
finding disposition and refinement. Tactical effects can reopen strategic understanding.

This is an explanatory reading of SCAR, not a one-to-one mapping of its six positions.
Observe and Orient recur during framing and assessment; Decide is not only acceptance.
Review feedback does not create a compulsory third OODA loop.

## Example: ordinary repair

Frame at 1, build at 2, observe a defect at 3, adjudicate at 4, repair at 2,
reassess at 3 and resolve readiness at 4. Only designated authority permits 5;
record at 6. The OODA vocabulary adds no transition.

## Example: intent-breaking observation

Evidence at 3/4 invalidates an assumption. Reopen 1 within intent; if bounds or
intent materially change, backbrief the commander. Do not bypass acceptance authority.

## Limits

The existing lifecycle governs. This account does not certify an exact canonical
Boyd-to-SCAR derivation; no original Boyd source was freshly read in this research.
OODA is already proposed locally and therefore is not one of the five unused additions.
