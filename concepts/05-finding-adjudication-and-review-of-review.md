# 05 — Finding adjudication and review-of-review

Status: existing SCAR mechanism; criticism alleges rather than automatically edits.

## Mechanism

At lifecycle position 4, give each finding a stable ID and exact candidate or review
target identity/location. Record allegation, criterion, consequence, priority,
evidence, counterexample and disputed interpretation. Name the decision-maker.

Decide Supported, Unsupported or Unresolved with evidence-based rationale.
Repair supported changes with a new revision; reject unsupported allegations;
record unresolved effects. Refresh affected evidence and conclusions.

Review-of-review is targeted by concrete doubt: unsupported evidence,
contradiction, threatened obligation, disputed interpretation or consequential gap.
There is no compulsory recursion depth or repeated clean-pass requirement.

## Example

A reviewer demands a daemon shutdown test from an offline presentation.
The decision-maker inspects applicability and rejects the unsupported requirement;
the correct response is not to add irrelevant machinery merely to satisfy criticism.

## Limits

Disagreeing with a reviewer does not itself refute a finding. A required unresolved
finding cannot be relabeled ready without disposition. Simple failures can have
compact records; adjudication need not create a separate document per finding.
