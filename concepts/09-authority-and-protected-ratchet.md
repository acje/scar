# 09 — Authority and protected ratchet

Status: existing SCAR acceptance mechanism.

## Mechanism

Name drafter, reviewer, finding decision-maker and designated acceptance authority,
including overlaps and limits. The drafter cannot self-authorize acceptance.
Standing authorization may avoid repeated human decisions but not exactness duties.

Compare protected obligations individually and compatibly. An average improvement
cannot hide a required failure. Unknown comparability blocks old-contract acceptance;
resolve it or separately authorize a changed contract without claiming preservation.

Acceptance authorizes exact candidate content, expected main baseline, scope and
criteria/evidence context. Changes invalidate affected authorization. Recheck before
the authorized commit, then confirm resulting HEAD and exact content.

## Example

An optimizer improves average accuracy but removes a mandatory accessibility witness.
The score cannot authorize acceptance. Preserve the required obligation or seek an
explicitly changed contract; do not claim the old protection still holds.

## Limits

Edits-only verified work is not Accepted. Scratchpad records corroborate but cannot
substitute for an actual authorized commit. Denied, stale or interrupted acceptance
is Unresolved. Corrections append authorized commits rather than erasing decisions.
This is protocol authority, not a claim of implemented runtime security.
