# 10 — Systems thinking and leverage points

Status: bounded external inspiration used in SCAR framing, not an imported algorithm.

## Mechanism

Look beyond parameter tweaks to the structure producing outcomes: information flows,
rules, self-organization, goals and paradigms. Meadows's revised twelve-point list
offers leverage categories, explicitly not a recipe guaranteeing an intervention.

SCAR framing prioritizes consequential structures, symmetries and invariants with
witnesses and counterexamples before polish. That ranking is a SCAR author adaptation,
not a quoted Meadows technique or experimentally established leverage hierarchy here.

## Example

Repeated unsupported findings may come from missing source identity rather than
insufficient review rounds. Investigate the information-flow defect before adding
more reviewers; demonstrate the specific causal witness instead of assuming leverage.

## Limits

A high-level category does not identify the correct intervention by itself.
Changes still require scope, evidence, bounded work and acceptance authority.
Earlier nine-point numbering differs from the revised twelve-point list.
No newly read Final Caution passage or universal performance claim is used.
