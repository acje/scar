# 04 — Evidence derivation and freshness

Status: existing SCAR mechanism.

## Mechanism

Bind a conclusion through the entire chain:

criterion → exact target/base/criteria/context → witness locator and result
→ witness-to-conclusion reasoning → uncertainty and applicability.

A citation alone is not reasoning. State which passage or observed result supports
which claim, under which identity and conditions. Distinguish observation,
interpretation, local adaptation and untested effectiveness.

Candidate, baseline, criteria or evidence changes invalidate affected conclusions.
Refresh affected checks and derivations, including corrected reviews of an unchanged
candidate. Reuse needs unchanged exact content/context and an explicit reason.

## Example

An abstract supports that a method uses a surrogate, not its acquisition formula.
Describe the mechanism at abstract level and disclose the unread paper body.
A later source correction changes the derivation even if the candidate stays identical.

## Limits

A SHA-256 establishes byte identity, not truth. A successful packaging test establishes
its tested contract, not substantive source support or successful user behavior.
Unavailable evidence stays Unknown where required; do not infer a negative fact.
