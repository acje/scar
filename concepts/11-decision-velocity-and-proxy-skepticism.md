# 11 — Decision velocity and proxy skepticism

Status: bounded Bezos inspiration compatible with SCAR, not imported numeric policy.

## Mechanism

Distinguish the intended outcome from process proxies. A completed form, many review
rounds or a green packaging check is not the thing the work was meant to achieve.
Reversible two-way decisions can use lighter process; consequential misalignment
should be escalated rather than hidden by prolonged local iteration.

SCAR applies task-proportional framing and coherent increments while retaining
required evidence and exact authority. Its mission command permits bounded local
adaptation, not a general evidence waiver for speed.

## Example

A library passes filename checks but misstates a paper's guarantees. The packaging
proxy is green while accuracy remains violated. Correct the source claim and refresh
the assessment rather than presenting speed or check count as success.

## Limits

Bezos's qualified roughly 70% information advice is not a SCAR threshold.
SCAR's 80% work-consumption signal concerns a different quantity.
No mandatory two-way-door taxonomy or disagree-and-commit rule is imported.
Decision speed does not prove correctness or excuse protected failures.
