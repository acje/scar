Add applicable concern rows (audience usability/accessibility, security, maintainability) with the same criterion/evidence fields; justify applicability or omission. No extra universal requirements or mathematical independence are implied. Optional scoped lens rows (for example the six optional lenses) are indexing/diagnostic categories requiring explicit applicability; they are evidence-seeking prompts, never diagnosed failures, and never add universal verdict rows, seats or obligations. Select concerns/lenses by scoped applicability; binding task/protected criteria cannot be waived by omission, and lenses supply evidence-seeking prompts that themselves create no extra obligations. Unsupported subjects and unavailable required guidance/checks remain Unknown; required Unknown blocks acceptance.
